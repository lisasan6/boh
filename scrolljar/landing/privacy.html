Privacy

Your email,
and nothing else.

The only thing this website collects is the email address you choose to give us. We use it for one thing: telling you when ScrollJar is ready.

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who we are

ScrollJar is run by [full legal name], [city, country]. Postal address: [postal address]. For anything about your data, write to hello@scrolljar.app. A real person answers.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it into a waitlist form, along with which waitlist you joined (ScrollJar, or Families).
  • Your messages, if you email us, for example about a Teams pilot. We use them to reply.

That's all. No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels. The fonts are served from this site, so visiting it doesn't tell Google or anyone else that you were here. The live app on the page runs entirely in your browser: it stores nothing and sends nothing.

What we use it for

One email when ScrollJar launches. If you joined on the Families page, one email when Families opens instead. We never sell, rent or share your address, and we never use it for ads.

For the waitlist we rely on your consent: you gave us your email to hear about the launch, and you can take that back at any time. When you email us, we use your message only to reply, because answering you is in both our interests. Short-lived server logs are kept for the same kind of reason: keeping the site running and safe.

Who handles it for us

  • Formspree receives the waitlist form and stores the sign-ups for us. Like any web request, a sign-up also carries technical details such as your IP address and browser. Formspree is based in the United States, so that's where your email is stored. Sending it there is covered by [transfer safeguard]. Here is Formspree's privacy policy.
  • [web host] serves these pages and, like every host, may keep short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use those logs for anything else.

If we send the launch email through an email service, we'll name it on this page before we use it.

How long we keep it

Until we've sent the launch email, and never more than 12 months after launch, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If ScrollJar never launches, we delete the whole list.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time, by writing to hello@scrolljar.app, to:

  • see the data we hold about you, or get a copy you can take elsewhere;
  • correct it or delete it;
  • stop or pause how we use it, or object to it;
  • take back your consent. That's as easy as giving it: every email we send has a one-click way out.

If you're in the EU or the UK and unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; in the UK, the ICO).

Kids

The waitlist is for adults. If you're under 18, ask a parent to join instead.

The app

This page covers the website. The ScrollJar app will have its own privacy policy before it reaches the App Store. It's built on Apple's Screen Time framework, which means we can't see which apps you block.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change affects how we use your email, we'll ask you first.
