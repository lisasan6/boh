For companies · ScrollJar for Teams

Your company pays
when people win.

A wellbeing benefit for companies. You pledge a budget to a cause your people vote for. Each person picks one hour a day that's theirs, and every hour they keep scroll-free unlocks part of the pledge. If someone slips, nobody pays.

  • Easy to runOne invite link to share. No employee lists to upload, nobody to monitor.

Any hour counts

Before a shift, between shifts or after work. One hour a day, the same chance for everyone, and pauses for on-call days or hard weeks.

Nobody is watched

No names, not even who joined. Groups under five stay hidden, totals are weekly, and there's nothing to export. It isn't a setting: the product can't show a person.

Nothing comes back as savings

Whatever isn't unlocked rolls into your next campaign, so the next jar starts partly full. Every unlocked dollar reaches the cause, with receipts.

Questions from HR

The honest answers.

Is joining mandatory?

Never. Joining is always each person's own choice, and they can leave any time. You won't see who joined or who left.

Does it track work time?

No. Each person picks one hour a day that's theirs, at any time that suits their life. Work hours never count, and nobody sees which hour anyone picked.

What happens to money that isn't unlocked?

It rolls into your next campaign, so the next jar starts partly full. It never comes back to you as savings. Everything unlocked goes to the cause, with receipts.

What do we need to set up?

A budget, a shortlist of causes and an invite link to share in Slack or email. No employee lists, no IT integration.

What does the pilot cost?

The software is free for a 6-week pilot with up to 50 people. You choose the charity budget. We'll talk about pricing on the call, before anything is billed.
