For families · coming after launch

A family jar,
not a family spy.

One jar for the whole family. Grown-ups can put their own price on their apps. Kids never pay a cent, and nobody gets paid for scrolling less.

Kids never pay

A parent pledges instead. Every scroll-free hour your kid keeps unlocks part of it for a cause they choose. Their good hours become their gift.

A treat for everyone

Add a shared goal if you like, such as a cinema trip when the jar fills. Everyone's hours count, grown-ups' too, so nobody is paid and nobody is blamed.

Private where it matters

The family sees the jar, not each other's screens. Diaries stay personal, and nobody gets a report on anybody.

Try it: tap Mia's view
The family jar

One jar,
two kinds of coin.

The gold coins come from the grown-ups, at the price each of them set on their own apps. The two-tone coins are the kids' scroll-free hours: each one unlocks part of a parent's pledge for the cause that kid chose.

  • a grown-up's coin
  • unlocked by a kid's hour

Switch to Mia's view to see what a kid sees: their own hours and their cause, nobody's screens. Then preview the tip-out: the lid pops open, every coin flies off to the causes, and the jar spins with joy.

How it will work

Four steps, no reports.

  1. 1A parent pledgesa monthly amount, paid only for the hours the kids unlock
  2. 2Everyone pickstheir own apps, and kids pick their cause
  3. 3Coins fill the jargrown-ups' coins, and a two-tone coin for each hour the kids unlock
  4. 4The jar tips outand each kid sees the cause their hours went to
No cash for kids

Paying children to scroll less crowds out their own reasons. Their hours become a gift instead.

No reports on anybody

The family sees the jar together, not each other's screens or apps.

No reading diaries

Everyone's diary stays theirs, grown-ups' and kids' alike.

No charges to kids, ever

Under-18s are never charged. A parent's pledge is the only money involved.
