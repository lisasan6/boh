# Film scenes: prompts for an image generator

These prompts are for the "Sound familiar?" film on the website. They're written as prose, which is how current image generators read best. They work in ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney or Ideogram.

## How to use them

1. **Make the anchor first.** Paste the style block followed by scene 1 (Bed). Generate about 4 versions and pick the one that looks most like your references (the 1930s Mickey stills). It sets the look for all the others.
2. **Make the other eight with the anchor attached.** Upload the anchor image and start the prompt with: *"Use the attached image as the style reference: same line work, same greys, same eyes and proportions, same film look."* Then paste the style block and the scene. This keeps all nine consistent.
3. **Fix small problems by editing, not regenerating.** If an image is nearly right, ask for one change and list what must stay. For example: *"Change only her expression to a dreamy grin. Keep everything else unchanged: pose, bed, clock, framing, line style, greys."* If the composition is wrong, generate again instead.
4. **Send me the nine images.** I'll crop them to the film frame, add the flicker, grain, weave and subtitles, and compress them for the web.

Before publishing, check that the generator's terms allow commercial use. Paid plans usually do. Ask for the largest size it offers in landscape: 16:9 or 3:2 is fine, because I crop.

**If it colours other things** besides the phone, ask for a fully black-and-white image instead. I'll add the mint glow on the phone myself.

## Style block (paste before every scene)

> An original black-and-white still from an early-1930s rubber-hose cartoon short, as if photographed from a 35mm print. Characters are ordinary people drawn with thin, even black ink outlines, bold solid blacks and clean whites. They have round heads, big white eyes with black pie-cut pupils, bendy rubber-hose arms and legs with bare four-fingered hands, and big rounded shoes. Expressions and poses are big and theatrical, with motion lines where something moves. Backgrounds are soft painted greys with airbrushed shading, detailed period-style props, wood grain and soft cast shadows on the floor. The lighting is a gentle studio key from the upper left, and light film grain sits over everything. The single touch of colour is the phone screen, glowing mint green (#3CF0CB), which casts a soft mint light on the face of whoever is looking at it; every other part of the image stays black, white and grey.
>
> Composition: a wide shot with the faces and main action in the upper two-thirds of the frame, and only floor or furniture in the bottom quarter, where subtitles will sit. 16:9 landscape.
>
> No captions, no watermark, no logos, no extra text.

## The nine scenes

1. **Bed, morning.** A young Black woman lies in bed under a white polka-dot duvet, her curly hair fanned across the pillow and a sleep mask pushed up onto her forehead. She holds her phone up above her face with one arm and wears a dreamy, half-asleep grin, heavy eyelids, eyes fixed on the screen. On the nightstand beside her, a round twin-bell alarm clock hops into the air and rings, with shaking lines around its bells. Morning light falls in stripes through window blinds.
2. **Coffee.** A middle-aged man with round glasses sits hunched at a small kitchen table, his nose almost touching his phone, mouth hanging open. In front of him, a mug of coffee has gone still and flat, and a slice of toast sits untouched on a plate. Behind him, a wall clock's hands spin in a blur of motion lines. Morning light from a window on the left.
3. **Bus.** A young East Asian woman wearing big headphones sits on a bus seat, staring down at her phone, completely absorbed. Outside the window behind her, the bus stop sign shoots past with long speed lines. A standing passenger holding the overhead strap turns toward her, wide-eyed, pointing back at the stop.
4. **Dog walk.** A young South Asian woman in a long dark coat walks along a park path, her eyes glued to her phone and her mouth dropped open. On the end of the lead, a cheerful white dog with black spots and floppy black ears pulls her forward, tongue out, chasing a butterfly. A squirrel on a tree trunk watches them both. Trees, a lamp post and soft clouds fill the background.
5. **Desk.** A young Latino man slumps in an office chair at a desk. His laptop screen is a blank white page, and a sticky note on its edge reads "DUE 5PM" (EXACT, verbatim, in bold hand-lettered capitals). He looks down at the phone in his lap, chin on his chest. A wall clock behind him shows almost five o'clock, and a little plant leans on the desk.
6. **Dinner.** At a family dinner table under a hanging lamp, a parent sits staring at their phone. Across the table, a small child kneels up on the chair and proudly holds up a crayon drawing of a house and a sun, waiting with hopeful eyes. The plates of food between them have gone still and cold.
7. **Film night, darker greys.** A couple sits on a sofa in a dark living room, lit by the glow of a television showing a mystery film: a shadowy figure in a hat in a doorway. The woman leans forward, wide-eyed, jaw dropped, a handful of popcorn frozen halfway to her mouth. Beside her, the man looks down at his phone, his face lit only by its mint glow. A black cat on the back of the sofa stares at the television too.
8. **Book.** An older white man with a bald head, white tufts of hair and a big bushy white mustache sits in a deep wingback armchair. Reading glasses sit on the end of his nose. A book lies open on his lap with a phone resting across the page, and he peers down at the phone over his glasses, eyebrows raised. A floor lamp glows beside him, and on a small side table a cup of tea steams.
9. **1 am, darkest greys.** In a dark bedroom at night, a person lies on their side in bed, the duvet pulled up, eyes wide open and tired, lit only by the mint glow of their phone. A bedside clock shows "1:07" (EXACT, verbatim). A crescent moon shows through the window, and the rest of the room fades into deep grey shadow.

## Moving versions (optional)

To make a scene move, use an image-to-video tool such as Runway, Kling, Pika, Veo or Sora on the chosen still. Ask for a 3–4 second seamless loop where only small things move: the clock hopping, the dog trotting, the TV flickering. Keep the camera locked off.

I can put short looping videos in the strip instead of stills. Each one adds roughly 0.5–1 MB to the page, so it's best for 2–3 scenes, not all nine.
