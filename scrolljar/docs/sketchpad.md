## 🤲 THE WOBBLE — resolved design (2026-07-30, L's call)

**The conflict it solves:** a buddy system where asking for help means interrupting your friend's own shield would make ScrollJar the thing that breaks people's focus. L's fix — *available friend talks; unavailable friend sends warmth instead* — removes it. Locked, with one addition: **the warmth must be the friend's own words, written in advance.** A canned line from us in a friend's place is a lie, and it's the one thing that would poison the whole feature.

**The Ladder of Who Speaks** — always in this order, we speak last and briefly:

1. **Your friend, live.** They're free. One quiet ping, they answer, a small exchange with quick replies. It closes itself after five minutes — a hand, not a chat app.
2. **Your friend, in advance.** They're in their own session, so we don't interrupt them at all. The asker gets the **standby note** the friend wrote in cold blood, dated ("she wrote this on 12 June, on a calm Tuesday"). The support is real and it's from a person.
3. **You, in advance.** No standby note exists. We say so plainly — *"he hasn't left a note yet, and we're not going to write one in his voice"* — and hand back the asker's **own words** from their diary. Self-initiated trigger, the strongest lever we have, and honest about whose voice it is.

**Non-negotiables inside it:**
- A wobble **can never unlock anything**. The coin still costs what you set. It's company, not a hall pass.
- The ping **cannot break the receiver's shield** — it's a passive delivery, not an interruption.
- **No read receipts, no response-time stats, no unanswered-wobble badge, no "you helped 12 times" score.** The receiver sees it later as *"you were reached for and your words showed up"* — never as something they missed. Support never becomes obligation.
- **One open wobble at a time.** Framed as focus, not as a limit.
- Closure is **opt-in from the asker only**: a single "tell her it landed." Not when, not why, not the outcome.
- Writing your standby note is prompted at buddy-jar setup — *"write it now, while you're calm"* — which makes reciprocity structural instead of nagged.

**Ship state:** built in prototype v2 (all three rungs + the standby-note editor, day and night). Entry points: the *instead* screen at the temptation moment, and the jar detail screen.

## 💡 Goal jars for friends and family (L, 2026-10-16) → designed as **Wish jars**, see spec §7 and the prototype
A shared jar for something the group wants: a holiday, a dinner out, a camera, a theme-park trip. Coins are **virtual**: ScrollJar keeps the score and **never touches the money**; when the goal is reached, people settle it among themselves.
- **The twist that makes it work:** the goal fills from **kept time** (days the deal held, or scroll-free hours), **not from slips**. If slips paid for the holiday, slipping would become the way to get the holiday, and the price would stop working. That's the reason "penalties to own savings" is in the parking lot below. Slips keep going 100% to charity as now.
- Fits the family rule already in place: rewards are shared and non-cash (a family treat), never money to a child.
- Open: pledge per kept day, per person; who sees what (the total, never who, as everywhere); what happens if the goal isn't reached (nothing is owed: it's a plan, not a debt).
