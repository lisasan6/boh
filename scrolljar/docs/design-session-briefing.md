# ScrollJar — Briefing for the Claude Design session

*You are designing the visual identity for ScrollJar. This doc is self-contained; deeper context lives in the same project (design-brief.md, scrolljar-spec.md, sketchpad.md, psychology-playbook.md).*

## What ScrollJar is (30 seconds)
A swear jar for scrolling. Users block their vampire apps (Instagram, TikTok…) at the OS level; opening one anyway costs a self-set price ($5) that drops as a coin into a jar — and the jar empties monthly to a charity they chose. 100% of penalties go to charity; the company earns membership only. Tiers: **Ghost Jar** (free) → **Jar Hero** ($5.99/mo · $49.99/yr) → **Jar Legend** ($9.99/mo · $79.99/yr, half of it donated by us). Tagline material: *"You can always choose distraction. It just shouldn't be free."* · *"We don't want your attention. We want it back in your hands."*

## The task
**Produce 2–3 clearly distinct visual identity directions**, then deepen whichever the founder picks. For each direction, design at minimum: (1) Home — the jar with coins, streak, commitments list; (2) the override sheet — "Open Instagram for 15 min — $5 → [charity]. Still want to?"; (3) Stats — hours returned + impact; (4) the paywall — Ghost/Hero/Legend. If a direction survives, extend to: group jar feed, breath-pause screen, rough-day check-in.

## Design direction — DUAL REGISTER (the core rule)
- **Core app register: vibrant, exciting, adult.** Committed, saturated color; bold modern energy (think Strava/Nike Run Club drive with Duolingo's warmth *grown up*). The founder's words: "vibrant and exciting… appealing, and not remind of a kindergarten." NO pastel-baby aesthetic, no cutesy overload.
- **Support register: calming.** The breath pause, rough-day check-in, and help/resources screens use a serene sub-palette (soft teal on warm cream, low saturation, slow 4–6s breathing motion). Science: saturation drives arousal — we want arousal on progress screens, low arousal exactly where self-regulation happens.

## Non-negotiables (from research; keep in every direction)
- **Curved everything** — cards 20–24px radius, pill buttons, no sharp vertices (angular = threat associations).
- **Amber/gold for broken commitments, NEVER red.** A coin drop is warm and informational, not an alarm.
- **The jar is the mascot and the interface.** Sleek, glassy, charming — not babyish. It has a subtle idle "breathing" animation and thrives when the user succeeds; it never suffers or guilt-trips.
- **Coin Gold** stays as the currency accent. Confetti/celebration reserved for milestones (monthly tip-out) only; vivid celebration ≤10% of any screen normally.
- **Typography:** rounded-but-adult. Fredoka is under review as too childish — try **Sora, Outfit, or Space Grotesk** for display, with Nunito or Inter for body. Tabular numerals for money/stats.
- **Motion:** 100–400ms, ease-out; coin drop ~450ms with one soft settle bounce; honor reduce-motion.
- **iOS shield constraint:** the block screen that iOS shows allows ONLY: background color/blur, an icon, title, subtitle, and two text buttons. Design the shield within exactly that box.
- No leaderboards, no follower counts, no engagement-bait UI anywhere.

## Starting palette (evolve it — especially toward vibrancy)
Jar Teal #4DB6A5 (push deeper/more electric for the vibrant register), Coin Gold #FFC94D, Peach #FF9E7A, Lavender #B9AEEA, ink #25403C (never pure black), warm cream #FAF7F1 (support register base). Deep-toned/dark backgrounds are welcome for the vibrant register. Chart marks must pass color-blind checks (current validated pair: #0E9077 / #C07E17 on cream — revalidate on new surfaces).

## Voice for any copy in the mocks (The Voice Law)
Every string helps someone become who they're trying to be. Banned words: failed, wasted, guilty, shame. Use real lines: "You made a jar promise." · "I'll stay committed 💪" / "Open anyway · $5" · "No rush. The scroll will wait." · "Drop a coin & open" · "The jar smiled. Streak intact." · "Even your weak moments do some good in this world." Paywall trust line: "Your membership is what lets us give away 100% of every penalty."

## Three suggested directions to explore (feel free to propose your own)
1. **Electric Emerald** — deep near-black-green backgrounds, glowing teal + gold, glassy jar with light refraction. Nighttime-premium energy.
2. **Solar Warmth** — light but VIVID: hot gold, coral, saturated teal on warm white; the jar as a bright hero object. Optimistic daytime energy.
3. **Arcade Heart** — the boldest: saturated color-blocking (teal/gold/violet), chunky type, playful-but-sharp; Gen-Z gaming energy, zero childishness.

Deliver each direction as a mini-stylescape (palette, type, the jar rendered, one home screen) first — get the founder's reaction before building out screens.
