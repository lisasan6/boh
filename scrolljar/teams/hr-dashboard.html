What you can't see, by design

✕Names. Not who joined, not who didn't.
✕Any one person's evenings, or when anyone slipped.
✕Which apps anyone chose. Apple doesn't tell us either.
✕Work hours. They never count and never leave the phone.
✕Daily numbers. Weekly totals only, so nobody can be singled out.
✕Groups under 5 people, including survey answers.
✕Exports about individuals. There is nothing to export.
✕Employees' personal jars. Their own stakes and charity stay theirs.
✕Evening notes. They stay between colleagues, are anonymous by default, and are deleted when the campaign ends.

These aren't permissions you could switch on. The product has no screen, report or setting that shows a person, and our system never stores those details in a form that could.
